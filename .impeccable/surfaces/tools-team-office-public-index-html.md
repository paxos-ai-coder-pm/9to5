## Scope

The studio's main page (`tools/team-office/public/index.html`, Simple view and Behind the scenes) and the launch page that shares its stylesheet. Mode: **Operate**.

## Audience and job

The owner, at his own computer, checking in between other things: start a new idea, answer Maya, see what the crew is doing, open what it built. Pinned: the current layout (office + chat on top, projects + start card below, one screen without page scrolling on desktop), the pixel office and named team, sage `#9cb29e` + black `#000000` with one red for errors, light and dark themes, plain words, WCAG AA.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The studio as a workshop's commission book: every idea is a numbered commission, signed off station by station by the crew. It refuses the category default of a glowing dark dashboard with glass cards.

OWN-WORLD: Sage and black paper with crisp 1.5px black rules and square corners; depth only from the book as a physical object (sheets lifting off the desk with a soft, offset shadow; stamps, number wheels and pages moving in 3D) and never from glows, glass or gradient fills (amended at the owner's request, "Physical book" choice). Archivo, condensed for labels, numbers and stamps, normal width for reading. Status is a stamp in a fixed box: filled done, hollow waiting, struck red when blocked.

STORY: You see at a glance what's being built, who has signed it off, and what to do next; you start a new commission or open a finished one.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Black header band with the studio name and status; office screen edge to edge in its panel with who's who beside; Maya's chat as a black panel on the right; the commission book (numbered project entries with stamp strips) bottom left; the new commission card, its number and the idea box, bottom right, with "Send to Maya" as the primary action.

FORM: Workshop order book, 5th of 7 on the ordered list. seed:af8d066623f187c0

Signature interaction: a station's stamp falls in 3D, presses and leaves its ink the moment its step completes (the stepper, and a newly finished project's sign-off strip, one stamp at a time). Supporting motion: the start card's number on a numbering machine's wheels, new commission entries turning in like a page, the view switch turning the page. Reduced motion: no movement; stamps fade their ink in.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
